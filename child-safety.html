Child Safety Standards

Zawaj Islami is committed to protecting children and preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation.

Zero Tolerance

Our application has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation. Any content or behavior involving the sexual exploitation or abuse of minors is strictly prohibited.

Reporting

Users can report any suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation through the reporting and support channels available in the application. We take reports seriously and investigate them appropriately.

Cooperation

We cooperate with relevant authorities when required by applicable law and take appropriate action against accounts or content that violate our policies.

Contact

For questions or reports concerning child safety, please contact the designated support team through the contact information provided within the Zawaj Islami application.
